# Tennessee Grade 6 Science Curriculum Reference

## Source
User-provided curriculum content (embedded reference for project use).

## Unit Clusters and Standards

### Energy
- 6.PS3.1 Analyze sources of energy in a system and gather evidence that energy is conserved during transfers of kinetic, potential (elastic, gravitational, chemical), and thermal energy.
- 6.PS3.2 Use a model to gather evidence that system changes can be caused by transfers of sound or thermal energy (conduction, convection, radiation).
- 6.ETS1.2 Design, construct, and test a device that minimizes or maximizes thermal energy transfer.

### Ecosystems, Populations, and Energy
- 6.LS2.1 Use data to evaluate and communicate impacts of living and nonliving environmental variables on population size.
- 6.LS2.2 Construct an explanation predicting competitive, symbiotic, and predatory interaction patterns.
- 6.LS2.3 Use a model to explain energy transfer through a food web and energy pyramid.

### Ecosystem Health and Biodiversity
- 6.LS2.4 Explain ecosystem health using abiotic and biotic patterns across terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems.
- 6.LS2.5 Analyze evidence on invasive species effects in Tennessee and design mitigation solutions.
- 6.LS4.1 Explain how biodiversity changes affect human resources and ecosystem services.
- 6.ETS1.1 Design, evaluate, and improve a solution for maintaining biodiversity.

### Earth's Hydrosphere and Atmosphere
- 6.ESS2.1 Diagram oceanic and atmospheric convection patterns caused by uneven heating.
- 6.ESS2.2 Gather evidence that ocean currents are driven by solar thermal transfer and salinity differences.
- 6.ESS2.4 Model cycling of water through Earth's systems driven by solar energy and gravity.

### Earth's Climate
- 6.ESS2.3 Explain how atmospheric flow, geographic features, and ocean currents affect regional climate through heat transfer.
- 6.ESS2.6 Model greenhouse gases' role in regulating average surface temperature.
- 6.ESS2.7 Collect data showing how air mass interactions change local weather and support prediction.

### Earth and Human Activity
- 6.ESS2.5 Analyze impacts of humans and other organisms on water cycle, landforms, and atmosphere.
- 6.ESS2.6 Model greenhouse gases' role in regulating average surface temperature.
- 6.ESS3.1 Use data to explain renewable and nonrenewable resource consumption and sustainability impacts.
- 6.ESS3.2 Compare existing and emerging renewable/alternative energy technologies.
- 6.ESS3.3 Communicate impacts of human activities on the biosphere, including conservation and extinction.

## Additional Grade 6 Standards (Overview Text)

### 6.PS3 Energy
- Compare properties and sources of kinetic, elastic potential, gravitational potential, electric potential, chemical, and thermal energy.
- Explain potential and kinetic energy transformations.
- Show relationship between kinetic energy, mass, and speed.
- Investigate thermal energy transfer by radiation, conduction, and convection.

### 6.LS2 Ecosystems
- Environmental variables and population size.
- Competitive, symbiotic, and predatory interactions.
- Energy transfer in food webs and pyramids.
- Abiotic and biotic patterns across biomes.
- Invasive species effects and mitigation.
- Ecosystem change over time from physical, population, human, and natural causes.
- Auditory and visual communication in organism survival.

### 6.LS4 Biological Change
- Biodiversity change impacts on ecosystem stability and resources.
- Design solutions to maintain biodiversity while meeting human needs.

### 6.ESS2 Earth's Systems
- Ocean current causes (thermal transfer and salinity).
- Convection patterns from uneven heating.
- Climate effects from atmospheric flow, geography, and ocean currents.
- Human and organism impacts on hydrologic cycle.
- Weather prediction from maps, satellites, and radar.
- Weather and severe storms from interacting air masses and pressure systems.

### 6.ESS3 Earth and Human Activity
- Renewable vs nonrenewable resource sustainability.
- Renewable and alternative energy technologies.
- Human impacts on the biosphere.

### 6.ETS1 Engineering Design
- Evaluate constraints for ecosystem and biodiversity solutions.
- Design and test solutions that impact energy transfer.

## Design Theme for Game Alignment
- Core theme: energy across systems and scales drives ecosystems, Earth resources, oceans, weather, and climate.
- Literacy emphasis: students gather reliable evidence and build evidence-based arguments.
- STEM integration is expected as a stand-alone disciplinary core idea.
